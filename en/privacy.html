Memo Camera Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

"Memo Camera" (the "app") is an Android app made by behappybill, an individual developer (the "developer"). This policy explains how the app handles personal information.

1. Summary

  • The app sends no data to the developer's servers or anyone else's. The app has no internet permission.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, ads, location data or face recognition.
  • Your photos and notes stay on your device until you delete them.
  • Photos and notes leave the app only when you tap the share button and choose an app.

2. Information processed only on your device

InformationPurposeWhere it is kept
Photos taken in the app and small list images (thumbnails)Keeping each photo with its note and showing the listApp-private storage
Notes and capture timeDescribing photos, search, grouping by dateApp-private database
Settings (the "Save to gallery" choice on the camera screen)Running the appApp-private storage

The developer cannot access this information. The app does not request location permission, so it does not record location in photos.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used only to take photos. Requested when you first open the camera screen; you can change it in your device settings at any time.
  • The app does not request microphone, location, contacts or storage-read permission.

4. Saving to the gallery

If you turn on "Save to gallery" on the camera screen, or tap "Save to gallery" on a photo's detail screen, a copy of the photo is saved to the "메모 카메라" album in your device gallery. The copy does not include the note. Photos saved to the gallery follow your gallery app and its backup settings (for example, cloud photo backup). Deleting the app does not delete gallery copies.

5. Sharing and copying notes

When you tap "Share" on the detail screen, the app hands the photo and note to your device's share sheet, and the app you choose (a messenger, for example) receives them. What happens next follows that app's and service's policy; the app does not send them anywhere else. "Copy note" copies the note to your device clipboard. The app deletes the temporary photo copies it makes for sharing the next time it starts.

6. Backup

App data is not included in the device's automatic backup. If you change phones, the photos and notes inside the app do not move with you. Keep "Save to gallery" on for photos you need to keep.

7. Retention and deletion

Information stays only on your device until you delete it. Deleting a photo in the app deletes the photo, its thumbnail and its note; deleting the app deletes everything inside the app. Copies you saved to the gallery are not deleted.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and is made for users aged 16 and over.

9. Your rights

The developer holds none of your personal information, so you can access, correct, delete or move it yourself on your device (editing notes and deleting photos in the app, sharing, deleting the app).

  • South Korea (Personal Information Protection Act): the privacy officer is the developer, Seongjin Han, reachable at the email below.
  • European Economic Area and UK (GDPR): the developer does not collect or process your personal information. You have the right to complain to a supervisory authority.
  • United States (California and other state laws): the developer does not sell or share personal information.

10. Changes

If a feature such as cloud backup changes how information is handled, the developer will announce it in the app and on this page before it takes effect.
